Privacy and Data

A plain-language summary of what the app keeps. The formal documents are the Privacy Policy and the Data Security and Privacy Statement linked from the Marketplace listing.

The short version

The app stores configuration, requests, an audit trail and a small amount of space metadata. It stores Atlassian account ids to record who asked for a space, who approved it and who owns it. It stores no page content, no attachments, no names and no email addresses. Nothing leaves Atlassian.

What is stored

KindExamplesWhy
ConfigurationBlueprints, naming policies, approval rules, settingsThe administrator created them
RequestsProposed name and key, purpose, chosen owner, decision and reasonThe request, and the record of what was approved
Account idsRequester, approver, owner, blueprint creator, audit actorAttribution
Space metadataId, key, name, page count, last activity, review dateThe inventory
HashesRole assignments, labelsDrift detection without keeping a copy of your permissions
Audit trailWho did what, when, and the resultGovernance

What is never stored

Page content. Attachments. Comments. Display names. Email addresses. IP addresses. Anything from a product other than the Confluence site the app is installed on.

When a space is provisioned, the pages are copied by Confluence's own server-side copy task. The content does not pass through the app.

Where it lives

In Forge hosted storage, inside the region of your Confluence site. Matify operates no servers and holds no copy of your data. There are no subprocessors and no third-party services.

How long

Audit entries are kept for 24 months by default and an administrator can set anything from 6 to 60 months. Inventory snapshots keep the newest twelve per space. Blueprints, policies and requests stay until someone deletes them.

Deleting it

  • Delete individual blueprints, policies, requests or audit ranges from the admin console.
  • Settings → Delete all app data removes everything the app stores, behind a confirmation.
  • Uninstalling the app makes Forge remove its storage.

Spaces, pages, labels and role assignments the app created stay in Confluence after uninstall. They are your content, not the app's, and removing them would be destroying your work.

Personal data requests

The app implements Atlassian's personal data reporting interface. Atlassian can ask which account ids the app holds and instruct it to erase them; the app removes the id from its own records. It does not delete spaces or pages in response, because that content belongs to your organisation rather than to an individual.

For a request about data the app holds, talk to your Confluence administrator or write to mira.maty@web.de.

Logging

The app writes operational logs to Forge: request ids, space ids, step names, timings and error codes, with account ids where an action needs attribution. Logs never contain page content, space descriptions, the purpose text a requester typed, display names or email addresses.
