Space Blueprints for Confluence

Turn a space you already trust into a blueprint. Let your team request new spaces through a form that enforces your naming policy. Approve with one click. The app creates the space with the blueprint's access configuration, copies the page tree, assigns an owner and a review date, and keeps a record of all of it.

What it does

  1. An administrator marks an existing space as a blueprint, and sees up front what a copy cannot carry across.
  2. Anyone eligible requests a space. The form validates the name against your naming policy before it will submit.
  3. An administrator approves, or an auto-approval rule handles it.
  4. The app provisions: creates the space with the blueprint's access configuration, assigns the owner their role, copies the page tree using Confluence's own server-side task, applies labels, and records the blueprint and a review date.
  5. The inventory keeps every space accounted for, and flags drift when one stops matching its blueprint.
  6. The audit trail records who asked, who approved, and what happened.

Trust

The app runs entirely on Atlassian's platform and makes no external network calls of any kind: no analytics, no error reporting, no content delivery network, no remote fonts. It stores metadata and Atlassian account ids, never page content. It never deletes or archives a space.
