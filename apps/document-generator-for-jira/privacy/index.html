Privacy policy — Document Generator for Jira

Publisher: Matify · App: Document Generator for Jira (Word, PDF & Excel) (Atlassian Jira Cloud) · Contact: support@matifyapps.de Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Document Generator for Jira app handles data when a Jira Cloud site installs it from the Atlassian Marketplace. The app turns Jira work items into Word, PDF and Excel documents based on Word templates. The organisation that installs the app decides who may generate documents and how the app is configured.

The app is built on Atlassian Forge. Atlassian's handling of your Jira site and of Forge hosted storage is governed by Atlassian's own privacy policy.

In short: no Jira content or personal data leaves Atlassian. Matify sees only anonymous usage counts in Forge logs, and only if your admin allows app log access.

2. Products

The app is installed into Jira Cloud and works on the Jira work item view, the issue navigator, its own pages under Apps and Jira settings. It processes data only from the Jira site it is installed on.

3. What the app processes

Jira data, read with the permissions of the person generating a document The work item fields a template uses, rich text (descriptions, environment, multi-line custom fields), comments when the person chooses to include them, linked work items, sub-tasks and child work items, attachment names and sizes, and the contents of image attachments (PNG and JPEG) that a template places in the document. Also: the site's list of fields, the person's language, time zone and group memberships, their Jira permissions, and saved filters and projects they can see.

Templates The Word files Jira administrators upload, with any content they contain, together with the list of placeholders, the fields to read and the validation report.

Atlassian account IDs The account ID of each person who starts a background document is stored with that document's record. The collected data of a background document (section 9) can also contain people's display names and the account IDs of people @mentioned in rich text or comments, because they are part of the Jira content the template uses. The app stores no e-mail addresses or avatars.

Not stored Generated documents, image contents, passwords, API tokens or other credentials, IP addresses, device data and analytics.

4. Why

Only to provide the app's functions to the installing organisation: validating and previewing templates, generating documents in the user's browser, running large documents in the background, attaching documents to work items on request, enforcing the administrators' settings and limits, protecting Jira's shared API limit, and showing administrators anonymous usage counts.

5. Generated documents

Documents are built in the browser of the person who generates them and saved on that person's device. They contain whatever Jira data the template selects, which can include people's names (for example the assignee), and a template can include the name of the person who generated the document. The app does not store generated documents. Your organisation controls these files.

If a person clicks Attach, the document is added to a work item as a normal Jira attachment. It is then Jira data under your site's permissions and retention, and it stays in Jira when the app is uninstalled or its data is deleted. Attaching is the only change the app makes in Jira.

6. Background documents

For documents above the direct limit, the app collects the Jira data in the background and keeps it until the person renders the document in their browser. It reads Jira as the person who started the document, through Atlassian's offline user impersonation, and never as anyone else. Jira applies that person's current permissions to every read. Only that person can read the collected data.

7. Where the data is stored

Only in Forge hosted storage on Atlassian's infrastructure, separate for each installation. Matify runs no server or database of its own and has no access to the stored data.

Data residency: Forge hosted storage follows the Jira site's data residency location and moves with the site.

  • In scope (stored in Forge hosted storage under your data residency): templates and their versions, placeholder lists, field plans and validation reports; background document records; collected data of background documents; settings.
  • Out of scope: usage counters and API budget records (they contain no end-user data), and the anonymous log lines described in section 13.

8. Sharing, egress and subprocessors

The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform; no data leaves Atlassian. It declares no external connections, no remote backend and no web triggers, and uses no analytics, telemetry, error-reporting or e-mail service. Its pages load no external scripts, fonts or images. The only subprocessor is Atlassian, as the provider of Jira and the Forge platform. Matify does not sell or share data.

10. Deletion

  • People can delete the collected data of their own background documents at any time (Delete now or Cancel).
  • Jira administrators can delete templates and versions, cancel or delete any background document, and use Delete all app data (typed confirmation), which removes all templates and versions, background documents and collected data, usage counters, settings, caches and API budget records. Documents attached to work items stay in Jira.
  • A daily cleanup deletes data at the end of the retention periods in section 9.
  • When the app's subscription ends, no new documents can be generated, the collected data of queued, running and ready background documents is deleted, and administrators can still download, archive and delete templates, delete background documents and use Delete all app data.
  • Data the app deletes is removed from its storage at once; Atlassian may keep copies for a limited time under its own data lifecycle.
  • Uninstall: the app cancels open background documents and deletes their collected data. All other app data follows Atlassian's data lifecycle for uninstalled Forge apps: it is retained for 28 days after the uninstall and then deleted. A reinstallation is a new installation, and data is not automatically restored. Download the templates you want to keep before uninstalling. Nothing is exported or sent to Matify on uninstall.

11. Personal data, closed accounts and requests

The organisation that installs the app is the controller of its Jira data. Matify processes the app's data in Forge hosted storage only on that organisation's behalf, and processes nothing outside Atlassian.

The app uses Atlassian's personal data reporting API: once a week it reports the account IDs it stores. When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app deletes that person's background document records and collected data. This also runs when the app's subscription has ended.

Requests to access, correct or delete personal data held in the app should go to the organisation that administers the Jira site. Matify will help that organisation; contact support@matifyapps.de.

12. Security

  • Every function checks the signed-in person's permissions on the server; administrator functions need the Jira permission Administer Jira.
  • Every Jira read runs as a person: the signed-in user, or for background documents the person who started them. The app makes no Jira calls with its own identity.
  • Templates with macros, encryption, external links, links to files, network shares or programs, mail-merge data sources, dangerous field codes or embedded objects are rejected, and every filled Word document is checked again before it can be downloaded or attached.
  • Excel output is protected against formula injection.

Details: the data security and privacy statement and Permissions and security.

13. Operational logs

The app writes technical logs on the Forge platform: event names, error codes, counts, durations and random reference numbers. They contain no names, account IDs, e-mail addresses, work item keys, JQL or work item content. For every completed document the app writes one anonymous line in exactly this form:

{"evt":"dgj.doc","fmt":"pdf","entry":"navigator","tpl":"pack:status-report","items":42,"ms":5300,"v":"1.0.0"}

evt is always dgj.doc; fmt is the format; entry is where the document was started (issue, navigator or page); tpl is the curated pack used, or custom; items is the number of work items; ms the build time in milliseconds; v the app version. Matify can see these logs only as far as Atlassian makes them available to app developers, and site administrators can turn off log access for the app.

14. Cookies

The app sets no cookies. Its pages store a few convenience flags (for example that a hint was dismissed) in the browser's local storage.
