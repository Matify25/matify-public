Privacy and data

No Jira content or personal data leaves Atlassian. Matify sees only anonymous usage counts in Forge logs, and only if your admin allows app log access.

Document Generator runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It has no server of its own, declares no external domains and uses no analytics, telemetry or error-reporting service. The formal texts are the privacy policy and the data security and privacy statement.

Where documents are made

Documents are built in the browser of the person who generates them and saved on their device. The app does not store generated documents. If someone attaches a document to a work item, Jira stores it as a normal attachment — it is then Jira data, governed by your Jira permissions and retention, and it stays when the app is uninstalled.

Generated documents contain whatever Jira data the template selects — for example assignee names — and {{doc.generatedBy}} writes the name of the person who generated the document. Your organisation controls these files.

Personal data

The app stores personal data:

  • the Atlassian account ID of each person who starts a background document;
  • for 24 hours (after the document is ready, or after it failed), the names, account IDs of @mentioned people and work item content that appear in the collected data of a background document;
  • whatever personal data Jira admins put into uploaded templates.

Personal data reporting: once a week (on Mondays) the app reports the account IDs it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API. When Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app deletes that person's background documents — records and collected data. This runs also when the app is not licensed.

Requests to access or delete personal data should go to the organisation that administers your Jira site. Matify will help that organisation; contact support@matifyapps.de. Matify cannot see the data stored in your site.

Attached documents are Jira attachments and are not deleted by the app.

Data the app deletes is gone from its storage at once. Atlassian may keep backups of Forge hosted storage for disaster recovery under its own data lifecycle.

Uninstall: the app cancels open background documents and deletes their collected data (best effort). All other app data follows Atlassian's data lifecycle for uninstalled Forge apps: it is kept for a limited period (28 days at the time of writing) and then deleted. A reinstallation is a new installation; do not count on your templates coming back. Download the template versions you want to keep before you uninstall, or use Delete all app data first.

Data residency

Forge hosted storage is data-residency enabled: the app's data stays in, and moves with, your Jira site's data residency location. No configuration is needed.

What Matify can see

Matify has no access to your site's data. Matify can read the app's Forge logs only as far as Atlassian makes them available to app developers, and your site admins can turn off log access for the app.

The logs contain event names, error codes, counts, durations and random reference numbers — no names, account IDs, e-mail addresses, work item keys, JQL or work item content. For every completed document the app writes one anonymous line in exactly this form:

{"evt":"dgj.doc","fmt":"pdf","entry":"navigator","tpl":"pack:status-report","items":42,"ms":5300,"v":"1.0.0"}
FieldMeaning
evtAlways dgj.doc
fmtFormat: docx, pdf or xlsx
entryWhere it was started: issue (work item), navigator (search) or page (Documents page)
tplpack: plus the pack id, or custom for your own templates (never the template's name)
itemsNumber of work items
msTime to build the document, in milliseconds
vApp version

Matify uses these lines only to count documents per format and entry point and to spot failures.
