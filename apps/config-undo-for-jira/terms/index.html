Terms — Config Undo for Jira

Config Undo for Jira is licensed under the standard end user agreement that Atlassian provides for Marketplace apps. You accept it when you install or subscribe to the app through the Atlassian Marketplace, where Atlassian shows its current text.

Provider-specific terms

Matify adds only the following. Each describes something the app actually does.

  1. Changes to your Jira configuration are made only on your administrators' instruction. Config Undo changes Jira configuration only when a Jira administrator on your site has turned on restore for that type of configuration, a restore was previewed and requested with a reason, and it was approved — by a second Jira administrator, or by the requester with a typed confirmation if your administrators chose single-admin mode. The only other change is the guided safe test, which creates a permission scheme named "Config Undo test …", restores it and deletes it again. Your administrators decide what is restored and when.
  2. Restores are limited to what Jira's APIs allow, and the app reports the result. Config Undo restores configuration through Atlassian's public Jira Cloud REST APIs. Some changes cannot be restored or are restored with differences; they are listed in the app's known limitations and shown in the preview before a restore runs. After every restore the app re-reads the object and reports whether it matches the selected version. Review the preview and the result before relying on a restore.
  3. Support. Support is provided by email at support@matifyapps.de, through the login-free support form and through the public tracker linked from the support page, under the Matify service level agreement. Do not include credentials, personal data or confidential content in a public ticket.
