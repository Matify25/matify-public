The customer remains solely responsible for making any change to their own Jira configuration; the App does not make such changes on the customer's behalf, whether automatically or otherwise.

5. No warranty that findings are exhaustive

This is important. The App reports what is visible through the Jira Cloud REST API, subject to the documented limitations in the published Known Limitations page in the app documentation. In particular, and without limiting that document:

  • The App cannot list the customer's Jira site administrators, because

Jira Cloud exposes no API for global permission grants.

  • The App does not identify who made a configuration change or exactly

when.

  • The App does not evaluate team-managed (next-gen) project access.
  • A configuration change made and reverted between two scans is not

detected.

  • Collection is subject to documented caps on very large sites.
