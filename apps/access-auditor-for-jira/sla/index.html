Service Level Agreement

Product: Access Auditor for Jira Publisher: Matify Applies to: Jira Cloud installations obtained through the Atlassian Marketplace Last updated: 2026-10-05

This page sets out what support you can expect from Matify, how quickly, and what this agreement does not cover. It applies to every installation — paid, trial and the free tier — because there is one support queue, not several.

1. How to reach support

Two routes, and they are equal. Use whichever suits you.

RouteAddressNotes
Public trackerOpen a ticketReading existing tickets needs no account. Opening one needs a free GitHub account.
Email<mira.maty@web.de>No account needed. The right choice for anything you would rather not write in public.

A suspected security vulnerability must never be reported in a public ticket. Email the address above with security in the subject line, or use the private reporting option on the tracker's security policy.

2. Hours of operation

Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time — CET in winter, CEST in summer — excluding public holidays and announced closures.

Every response time below is counted in these business hours. A ticket opened on Friday evening is first seen on Monday morning.

Planned closures longer than two business days are announced on the support tracker in advance, together with the date support resumes.

3. First-response times

A first response is a reply written by a person. It confirms the ticket has been read, states the severity it has been assessed at, and either answers the question or says what happens next. There is no automated acknowledgement, so the first reply you receive is always a real one.

SeverityWhat it meansFirst response within
CriticalThe app cannot be used by any administrator on the site, or a security vulnerability is suspected1 business day
HighA core function — scanning, findings, the permission explorer or exports — fails and there is no workaround2 business days
NormalSomething misbehaves but a workaround exists, or a question about configuration or about what a result means3 business days
LowA cosmetic issue, a documentation correction or a feature request5 business days

You choose the severity when you open a ticket. It may be adjusted after the first assessment, and the reply will say why.

4. Resolution

Matify does not promise a fixed resolution time for ordinary defects, because the cause decides what is possible. A good share of what is reported against an Atlassian Forge app turns out to be Jira configuration, a limit of what the Jira Cloud REST API exposes, or Atlassian platform behaviour — none of which a fix in the app can change. Promising a deadline for those would be promising something outside Matify's control.

What is committed instead:

  • For Critical and High tickets, a status update at least every two

business days until the problem is resolved or a workaround is in place.

  • Every ticket is closed with a reason: fixed and in which release, not

reproducible, working as designed with the explanation, or outside this agreement.

How fixes reach you

Fixes are released through the Atlassian Marketplace as a new version of the app. A fix that does not change the app's permissions reaches every installation automatically; there is nothing for you to install. A fix that needs a new permission requires a Jira administrator to approve the update, as Atlassian requires for any change of permissions.

5. Security vulnerabilities

Security reports are handled ahead of everything else.

  • Acknowledgement within 1 business day, the same as a Critical ticket.
  • Resolution follows Atlassian's

Security Bug Fix Policy for Marketplace apps. At the time of writing, that policy requires a vulnerability in a cloud app to be fixed within these periods of being reported or triaged:

SeverityCVSS scoreFixed within
Critical9.0 and above10 days
High7.0 and above4 weeks
Medium4.0 and above12 weeks
Lowbelow 4.025 weeks

Atlassian's policy is the authority. If it changes, the shorter of the two periods applies.

If a vulnerability affected customer data, the affected customers and Atlassian are notified.

6. Service availability

Access Auditor for Jira runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, inside your own Jira Cloud site. Matify operates no servers, no database and no service of its own that the app depends on.

The app is therefore available exactly when Jira Cloud and the Forge platform are, and that availability is Atlassian's. Matify makes no separate uptime commitment — it would be a promise about infrastructure Matify does not run.

Current platform status is published by Atlassian:

  • Atlassian Status — Jira Cloud and other products
  • Atlassian Developer Status — the Forge platform

Two properties of the app itself matter here. A scan that fails during a platform incident is simply retried on the next scheduled run. And a failed scan never triggers deletion, so a platform incident cannot erase your audit history.

7. What this agreement does not cover

  • Incidents in Jira Cloud or the Atlassian Forge platform. Report those to

Atlassian; Matify will help you work out whether a problem lies with the app or with the platform.

  • Jira Data Center and Jira Server, which the app does not support.
  • The access model of team-managed projects, and the other boundaries of the Jira

Cloud REST API described in the known limitations.

  • Changes to your Jira configuration. The app is read-only by design and never

writes to Jira, so it cannot fix a finding for you.

  • Custom development, integrations and on-site work.

8. Remedies

This agreement does not provide service credits, refunds or any other financial remedy. Refunds for Marketplace purchases are handled under the Atlassian Marketplace's own terms.

If Matify misses a response time in this agreement, the reply says so, gives the reason, and states when you can expect the next update.

9. Changes to this agreement

Material changes are announced on the support tracker at least 30 days before they take effect. A ticket is handled under the version of this agreement that was published when the ticket was opened.
